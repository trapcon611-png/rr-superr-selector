Shrunk Composite Pressure Rating (PRS) Architecture

PRS = 0.60 × z(PPAE_shrunk) + 0.20 × z(Delta_shrunk) + 0.20 × z(Consistency_shrunk)

Parameters are learned on 110 eligible batters and 130 eligible bowlers (≥100 pressure balls, ≥8 innings) across the full dataset of 888 batters and 647 bowlers, then applied to score the 20-player answer-sheet pool.
